# Working on your website

Everything happens in this one window. You never need to open anything else,
and you never need to copy or paste anything.

---

## The three things worth remembering

| You type | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| `/preview` | Opens your website beside the chat so you can look at it |
| `/publish` | Checks everything works, then puts your changes on the real website |
| `/undo` | Puts things back the way they were |

Everything else is just asking, in your own words.

---

## How to ask for a change

Talk to it like you'd talk to a person who's sitting next to you looking at the
screen. You don't need special words.

> "The green banner on the front page is too dark, can you lighten it a bit?"

> "On the booking page, put the price above the button instead of below it."

> "Add my photo to the about page." *(then drag the photo into the chat)*

> "The wording under 'Community Maker Space' doesn't sound like me. Change it to
> say it's for anyone who wants to learn, not just people who already sew."

> "Show me last month's income."

> "Add a new room called the Corner Office, $20 an hour, seats four."

If you don't like what it did, just say so — *"no, smaller"*, *"go back to how
it was"*, *"try a different green"*. You can go back and forth as many times as
you like. **Nothing you do in here touches the real website until you type
`/publish`.**

---

## Two useful habits

**Look before you publish.** Type `/preview` and click around the page you
changed. If it looks right, `/publish`. If not, keep asking for tweaks.

**Say what you want, not how to do it.** *"Make the events easier to read"*
works better than *"increase the font size to 18px"*. It knows the design of
your site and will keep everything matching.

---

## The parts of your site

**The public website** — the front page, About, Our Spaces, the community
calendar, and the pages where people book rooms or ask about counselling.

**Your admin area** — sign in and go to `/admin`. Only you can see this.

- **Rentals** — your tenants, what they owe, what's overdue, and the reminder
  emails that go out automatically
- **Bookings** — room booking requests to approve, and the calendar of what's
  booked
- **Finance** — income, expenses, and how each part of the business is doing
- **Counselling** — your appointments, your notes, and your to-do list. Nobody
  else can open this, not even someone signed in as a tenant.

## Things to ask Tyler for

There are a few things Claude deliberately won't do in this window, because
they involve passwords or bank details:

- Setting up or changing the Stripe account that takes card payments
- Adding passwords, keys, or bank information
- Changing who has admin access
- Anything to do with the domain name or hosting bill

If you ask for one of these, it'll tell you it's one for Tyler. That's expected,
not an error.

---

## If something looks broken

Say *"something's broken"* and describe what you were doing. It will look into
it and fix it if it can.

If it tells you something needs Tyler, believe it — it won't publish a broken
site, so the live website is still fine in the meantime.
